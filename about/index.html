About Places to Visit in Costa Rica

Updated November 10, 2026

Costa Rica is small, but its regions feel like different countries: volcano country around Arenal, cloud forest in Monteverde, Pacific beaches, the Caribbean coast and the wild Osa Peninsula. This guide explains each and how to string them together without spending your whole trip in the car.

Who publishes it

Places to Visit in Costa Rica is published by Eye To Ad Media, an independent web publisher in Denver, Colorado, run by Zach Wennstedt. We're not a tour operator or travel agent and can't book anything for you.

How we write it

  • Routes and drive times are planning estimates; mountain roads and weather change them.
  • Park rules and fees change, so we point to official sources rather than quoting prices that go stale.
  • No made-up reviews, ratings or prices.

How it's funded

Ads (Google AdSense), clearly labeled featured listings for local businesses, and Expedia affiliate commissions if you book a stay through our search. See the disclosure.
