Privacy Policy

Updated November 10, 2026

Places to Visit in Costa Rica (placestovisitincostarica.com) is a free guide to the country's regions, parks and beaches and how to link them into one trip. This page explains what is collected when you visit and how the site makes money.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, which sets cookies to measure visits: pages read, device type, approximate region and how you found us. We look at totals to decide what to cover next. Our host, GitHub Pages, may also keep basic server logs for security.

Messages

There are no sign-up forms on this site. If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

Opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at www.aboutads.info. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Expedia hotel search (affiliate disclosure)

Some pages include a hotel search from the Expedia Group affiliate program. If you search or click through, Expedia Group and its affiliate-tracking partners may set or read cookies on your device so a booking can be credited to this site, and we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Searches and bookings happen on Expedia under its privacy statement; we never see your booking or payment details. Commissions don't change what we write.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser. To ask about or delete a message you sent, use the contact page.

Children and changes

This site is for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect data from children under 13. Changes to this policy update the date above.
